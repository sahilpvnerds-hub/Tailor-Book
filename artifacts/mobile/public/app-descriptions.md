# Stitchix - App Store Descriptions

## Short Description (80 characters max)

## Full Description (4000 characters max)
Stitchix is a comprehensive tailor management application designed to streamline your tailoring business operations. Whether you're a small boutique or a large tailoring shop, Stitchix helps you manage everything from customer measurements to order tracking and invoicing.

### Key Features:

📏 Smart Measurements
- Capture and store detailed customer measurements
- Support for multiple measurement types (shirt, pant, suit, etc.)
- Photo attachments for reference
- Measurement history tracking

👔 Order Management
- Create and track orders efficiently
- Real-time order status updates
- Priority order handling
- Order history and search

💰 Invoicing & Payments
- Generate professional invoices
- Payment tracking and history
- Multiple payment methods support
- Automatic calculations

👥 Customer Management
- Customer database with profiles
- Contact information and preferences
- Customer history and notes
- Search and filter functionality

📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Business insights and statistics
- Revenue tracking
- Order analytics
- Performance metrics

🔔 Notifications
- Order status updates
- Delivery reminders
- Payment reminders
- Custom notifications

🌐 Multi-language Support
- Available in multiple languages
- Easy language switching
