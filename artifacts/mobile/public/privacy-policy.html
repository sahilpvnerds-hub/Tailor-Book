Privacy Policy for Stitchix

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Stitchix collects and processes the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details when you create an account
  • Business Data: Customer information, measurements, orders, invoices, and other business-related data you input into the app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, and performance data
  • Location Data: Optional location access for delivery address suggestions (only when you grant permission)
  • Media Files: Photos and images you upload (customer photos, measurement images, etc.)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Stitchix service
  • Process and manage your business operations (orders, measurements, invoices)
  • Send notifications about orders and updates
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission
  • Secure cloud storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Local data caching for offline access (encrypted on your device)

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (hosting, analytics, etc.)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account:

  • All your personal information will be permanently deleted
  • Your business data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained for legal or accounting purposes as required by law

7. Children's Privacy

Stitchix is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Permissions

Stitchix requests the following permissions:

  • Storage/Media: To save and upload photos (customer photos, measurement images)
  • Location: Optional - for delivery address suggestions
  • Notifications: To send order updates and reminders

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
